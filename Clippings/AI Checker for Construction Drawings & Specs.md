## How InspectMind works

A team of specialized AI agents working in parallel to catch issues humans miss.

STEP 01

### Ingest

Drop your drawings, specs, and codes. PDFs, DWGs, any format. We handle 10,000+ sheet plan sets across all disciplines.

2 minutes to upload, no setup required

STEP 02

### Cross-Check

5 specialized AI agents cross-reference architectural vs structural vs MEP vs civil simultaneously. Dimension alignment, openings vs beams, penetrations vs rebar.

All disciplines checked against each other

STEP 03

### Verify

AI verifies against IBC, ADA, fire/life safety codes and your project specs. Spots violations and spec-to-drawing mismatches.
